ClippedHQ

A personal automation tool that turns Twitch VODs into short highlight clips.

What it does

ClippedHQ watches a list of Twitch channels. When a tracked stream ends, it downloads the VOD, automatically finds likely highlight moments using chat activity and audio loudness, cuts short clips from those moments, and publishes them to connected platforms — including posting to TikTok on the operator's behalf via TikTok's Content Posting API.

Who runs it

ClippedHQ is operated by Tex for personal / small-scale use. It only connects to accounts that have explicitly authorized it through each platform's own login flow.
