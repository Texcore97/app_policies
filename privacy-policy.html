Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27.09.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ClippedHQ ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is a personal automation tool operated by Tex that monitors specified Twitch channels, generates short highlight clips from their broadcasts, and posts those clips to connected social media accounts (including TikTok) on the operator's behalf.

Who this applies to

The App is operated for personal / small-scale use and connects only to accounts explicitly authorized by their owner (the operator's own TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram accounts, or accounts belonging to someone who has given the operator permission to post on their behalf). The App does not offer sign-up to the general public.

Information we access

  • Twitch data: publicly available stream status and video (VOD) data for the channels configured by the operator, retrieved via the Twitch API.
  • TikTok account data: when the operator connects a TikTok account via TikTok's OAuth login, we receive an access token and refresh token that let the App upload video content to that account's inbox. We do not access TikTok data beyond what's required to post content (e.g. we do not read the account's private messages, contacts, or browsing activity).
  • Locally generated video files: downloaded stream recordings and the short clips generated from them.

How information is used

Access tokens are used only to authenticate requests to each platform's API in order to publish generated clips. Downloaded stream video is stored temporarily on the operator's own device or server while clips are being generated, and is deleted once processing finishes. We do not use any of this information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the App as described.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties. Data is sent only to the platforms the operator has explicitly configured (e.g. TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Twitch), solely for the purpose of reading public stream data and publishing the operator's own generated clips.

Data retention

Downloaded VOD files are deleted automatically once a clip batch has been generated and uploaded. Access/refresh tokens are stored locally on the operator's own device or server and are retained until the operator revokes access (e.g. by disconnecting the app in TikTok's account settings) or deletes them manually.

Your rights

Any account owner who has connected their TikTok account to the App can revoke that access at any time from TikTok's app-permissions settings. To request that any locally held tokens or data associated with your account be deleted, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone who does not meet the minimum age requirements of the platforms it connects to.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests regarding your data can be sent to clipped.hq.xyz@gmail.com.
